# Peer Endorsement Evidence Instructions (C2)

You must perform this part yourself because the rubric requires real, verifiable classmates and screenshots.

1. Open three classmates' LinkedIn profiles from your cohort.
2. Endorse only skills you can reasonably verify from class/project work, such as Statistical Analysis, Python, Data Visualization, or Machine Learning.
3. Capture a screenshot after each endorsement showing the classmate name and endorsed skill.
4. Save them as `Peer_Endorsement_1.png`, `Peer_Endorsement_2.png`, and `Peer_Endorsement_3.png`.
5. Place the screenshots in this folder before LMS submission.
6. If endorsements are reciprocal, keep screenshots/evidence of that as well.

Do not use random public accounts; the rubric requires same-cohort, relationship-appropriate endorsements.
